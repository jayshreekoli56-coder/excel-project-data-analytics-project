# 📊 Atliq Hardwares – Excel Business Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This Excel project analyzes the business performance of **Atliq Hardwares** across customers, markets, and fiscal years.  
The goal is to generate actionable insights related to **sales growth, profitability, market performance vs targets, and customer contribution** using Excel reporting and dashboards.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Excel Formulas (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF, Percentage Calculations)  
- Conditional Formatting  
- Data Cleaning & Reporting  

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The project uses multiple Excel reports exported in PDF format, including:

- Customer Performance Report  
- Market Performance vs Target Report  
- P&L Statement by Fiscal Year  
- P&L Statement by Markets  
- P&L Statement by Months  

**Time Period:** 2019 – 2021  
**Currency:** INR / USD (as per report)

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Customer-wise Net Sales comparison (2019–2021)  
- Market-wise performance vs sales targets (2021)  
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Net Sales Growth  
- Profit & Loss (P&L) analysis by:
  - Fiscal Year  
  - Market  
  - Month  
- Gross Margin & GM% trend analysis  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- 📌 Net Sales increased significantly from **87.5M (2019)** to **598.9M (2021)**  
- 📌 Gross Margin grew, but **GM% declined**, indicating higher cost pressure  
- 📌 India, USA, and South Korea are top-performing markets  
- 📌 Several markets failed to meet 2021 sales targets  
- 📌 Customer-level analysis highlights high-growth retail partners  

---

## 📊 Reports Included
- **Customer Performance Report** – Sales growth by customer  
- **Market Performance vs Target** – Target gap analysis  
- **P&L by Fiscal Year** – Overall profitability trends  
- **P&L by Markets** – Market-wise margin comparison  
- **P&L by Months** – Monthly & quarterly sales patterns
